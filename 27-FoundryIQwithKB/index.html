The big picture

From question to answer

Many AI apps follow a pattern like this:

  1. 01AskGive the app a question or task.
  2. 02ThinkA model or agent interprets the request.
  3. 03ConnectIt may use tools or retrieve knowledge.
  4. 04LearnInspect the answer and its evidence.

Not every lab uses every step.

A quick heads-up: use synthetic data only. Many exercises call live Azure services and can incur charges; each lab explains its own limits.
