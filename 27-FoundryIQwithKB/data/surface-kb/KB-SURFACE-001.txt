KB-SURFACE-001: Surface Laptop - external display triage
SYNTHETIC WORKSHOP DATA. Fictional organization runbook, not Microsoft support guidance.
Applies to: the workshop's Surface Laptop 5 and Surface Laptop 6 for Business fleet.
Procedure: Confirm that the internal screen works. Check monitor input and power. Test a known-good cable and a direct connection without the dock. Install organization-approved Windows, Surface, and dock firmware updates. Reconnect the dock and repeat sleep/resume twice.
Escalation: If a direct connection still fails with a known-good monitor and cable after updates, collect the dock model, OS build, display details, and reproduction steps for endpoint engineering.
Never ask the user to open the device or replace hardware solely on this runbook.
Related case: CASE-1001, resolved by approved dock firmware.
