CASE-1001: Surface Laptop 5 - external display disappears after resume
SYNTHETIC WORKSHOP DATA. Fictional case, not Microsoft support guidance.
Device: Surface Laptop 5; Windows 11; Workshop USB-C Dock A.
Symptoms: The internal display works, but an external monitor is blank after sleep.
Diagnosis: The workshop dock firmware was outdated; the same monitor worked when connected directly.
Resolution: Install approved Windows and Surface updates, update the approved dock firmware, reconnect the dock, and test sleep/resume twice.
Outcome: Two successful resume cycles; resolved in 25 minutes.
Related article: KB-SURFACE-001. Do not replace the laptop before testing a direct monitor connection.
